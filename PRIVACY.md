This privacy policy describes how the Jaidi Pan Shop mobile application (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects information.

## Information we collect

- **Phone number** – when you join the loyalty / rewards program
- **Name** – optional, when you register for rewards
- **Device push token** – to send order and promotion notifications (only if you grant permission)
- **Order details** – items you choose to send via WhatsApp (handled by WhatsApp, not stored by us unless you use future in-app ordering)

We do **not** collect precise GPS location, contacts, or photos unless a future feature explicitly asks and you consent.

## How we use information

- Display your loyalty stamp balance
- Send promotional or transactional push notifications (with your permission)
- Improve the App and customer experience

## Data storage

- Loyalty and menu data may be stored in Firebase (Google Cloud)
- Push tokens are stored so we can reach your device
- Cart contents are stored only on your device (AsyncStorage)

## Third parties

- **Firebase / Google** – database and optional messaging
- **WhatsApp** – when you choose to send an order; subject to WhatsApp’s own policy
- **Expo** – app updates and build infrastructure

## Your choices

- You can refuse notification permission; the App still works
- You can ask us to delete your loyalty profile by contacting the shop with your registered phone number
- Uninstalling the App removes local data on your device

## Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
